Fresh permanent point supply: 59 levels × 3 = 177; lifetime daily points = 20; five rescue receipts × 2 = 10. Total 207, with 240 available allocation slots. Specialized builds here spend 177 and are attainable without daily/rescue points. Forge ranks stop at 3. There are thirty Guardian receipts across Gloom 0–5; all-forty boon ranks and allocations >60 belong only to the retained legacy envelope.

Stacking order: base → forge → hero → effective level/stats → bounded boon ranks → final bounds. Damage above 50 approaches 100 smoothly; agility/tempo and movement use smooth diminishing returns; clip overflow becomes at most 1.5× impact. Crit overflow above 75% raises critical damage. The full legacy record survives.

Style analysis: for the identical encounter at night/day, permanent XP is identical at all seven style ranks. Old style scaling gave up to twice the XP per kill; it now pays up to the enemy's base coin value in carried bonus Sparks (rounded to whole Sparks, before pickup multipliers). Night retains up to 25% XP for extra encounter risk. Daily farming pays exactly twenty additional permanent points over 1000 or more dawns; repeat days keep banked currency rewards. Max-level XP converts at 20 XP per banked Spark with exact remainder. Currency and optional point conversion fund finite forge/hero/companion unlocks and paid choices/rerolls; no later increase in stats is granted.

Read the CSVs for starter weaknesses, ordinary/Guardian/King envelopes and all Gloom tiers. A starter facing the strongest King is a deliberately mismatched diagnostic, not campaign tuning. No claim of a human playtest, balanced enjoyment, controller review or target-hardware performance follows from this model. Authored contact timestamps retain existing action beats; this validates time agreement, not artistic judgment of the exact weapon pose.

Pace projection (`growth-pace.csv`): sequential full tower clears at Gloom 0–5 use the real deterministic FloorWave counts, guaranteed elite Guardians, two Slime children per BigSlime, and the King's actual fixed 600 XP. Excludes optional Wilds, missions, daily/rescue points, random elites and boss summons; assumes all enemies are killed once. Current and old D style both finish at level 40; old continuously-SSS style finishes at level 50. This measures the old upper-bound feedback loop, not an observed player's style history. The struggling-player core XP schedule is unchanged.
