## Controller and recovery behavior

Production buttons, sliders and boon cards register with one shared focus system. Directional input has a bounded repeat interval; confirmation activates at most one control per frame across GUI events. Sliders change in five-percent steps. Disabled choices are skipped, horizontal tabs stay on their row, page entry preserves the selected settings tab, and the new-journey confirmation defaults to **KEEP MY JOURNEY**.

The funded Guardian scenario exposed a reveal-time focus defect: the reroll was available while the cards were still locked. Reroll now shares the reveal lock, so first confirmation selects a boon instead of spending Sparks. The actual chapter route verifies this with carried currency available. Story/ending/stat/boon selections still call the durable production actions rather than replacing them with UI-only state.

Disconnect pauses active roaming and displays recovery instructions. Reconnect clears the notice without resuming automatically. Keyboard keys and mouse buttons switch help; pointer deltas generated by cursor locking cannot steal controller prompts. Dialogue advance and deliberate whole-dialogue/Timeline skip have distinct bindings. A frame fence prevents the button finishing a story from also interacting with the world.

The Wilds map suspends the live fight, movement and message reading timers. It draws as a modal sheet with an unobstructed legend, a focused close button, and controller/keyboard back paths. Closing it resumes without a stray dodge or another pause menu. Save-attention screens expose a controller close action and keep gameplay/flush attempts suspended while storage reports an error.

## Audio policy and score

World sound uses **12 sources**, including four reserved for threats; UI uses **four separate sources**. Foley cannot evict contact or threats. A stronger cue can replace a weaker voice; equal-priority pressure replaces the oldest peer. Each source owns one clip rather than stacking unbounded one-shots. Short repeated fuse pulses from the same position are limited. Scene construction stops previous-world voices.

Enemy/contact/projectile/player cues carry their emitter position. World sources use logarithmic attenuation (2–32 metres), zero Doppler and semantic priorities. Geometry filters covered sound at 3 kHz; covered threats retain more gain than ordinary effects. The quieter mix bounds cue peaks and reduces music gain; master zero mutes through the listener. Occlusion is sampled when a cue starts. This is not a moving acoustic simulation or a measured final mix.

The shared catalog has **33 IDs: 31 required recordings and two explicitly optional absent cues, hover/talk**. Build validation fails missing required assets, unreadable recordings, unsupported numbered takes and uncatalogued literal source IDs. Gaps are reported, and the runtime scans all slots 1–32 independently, so a missing second take cannot hide a valid third. Validation refreshes cached discovery.

The recorded Brushwick theme remains intact. Greenreach, Sunscorch and Hollow battle identities use paired generated bed/drive clips, each **8 seconds, 22,050 Hz, 120 BPM**. Drive adds independent percussion/lead data. Both layers share a scheduled DSP start and fixed pitch through hit-stop; a second bank enters scene changes on a bar with a one-second crossfade. Rapid requests remain bounded by the two banks. WAV previews and sample analysis are retained; preview peaks are below 0.242 and RMS is about 0.041. These are source-sample measurements, not a certification of the complete hardware mix.

## Comfort behavior

Reduced effects retain threat outlines, expanding ground markers, pose/action timing, hit words, health, scene fades, static bloom and ordinary particles. It suppresses the selected screen effects and automatic camera motion; it does not remove attack wind-up or collision protection.

Camera motion governs shake, impact shoves and FOV pulses. Auto camera governs orbit/arrival motion and lock framing; at zero, authored dialogue framing uses cuts rather than a dolly. The configured FOV remains steady during the strongest burst at zero motion. Individual flash, impact, line, aberration and vignette values are clamped independently. Render-time gating also removes an already-running selected effect immediately when its setting reaches zero. Ground-marker expansion remains a timing cue while rapid alpha pulsing and ready-state colour cycling are reduced.

The generated battle layers contain different percussion/lead material rather than a volume change to a single recording. The existing recorded town theme is not described as adaptive. The three battle environments use separate authored chord/pattern data. They are a technical score proof, not a claim of finished orchestration or a commissioned soundtrack.
